Privacy Policy

PocketCatGo — Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

PocketCatGo ("we", "us", or "our") is a real-world cat spotting game built for Android devices. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve the app:

  • Camera photos: When you take a photo of a cat, the image is processed on-device for cat detection and background removal. Photos are stored locally on your device by default. You have the option to share a sighting (photo and details) with the community map, which uploads it to our server.
  • Location data: Approximate location is recorded with cat sightings to place them on the shared map. GPS coordinates are obfuscated for privacy.
  • Device identifier: A stable device ID (Android Advertising ID or a random identifier) is used to distinguish sightings from different devices. This is not linked to your personal identity.
  • User-entered data: Cat names, descriptions, colors, behaviors, breed, and missing cat reports that you voluntarily enter.
  • App usage logs: Local log files for debugging purposes, stored only on your device.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • On-device processing: Camera images are analyzed locally using ML Kit for cat detection and background removal. Images are not sent to external servers for this processing.
  • Shared sighting map (optional): When you choose to share a sighting, cat sighting data (location, photos, descriptions) is uploaded to our API server to populate the community map. Your device ID is used to identify your sightings but is not displayed to other users. Sharing is opt-in — nothing is uploaded without your action.
  • App improvement: Local logs help us debug issues and improve the app experience.

4. Optional Google Sign-In

PocketCatGo works fully offline without any sign-in. If you choose to sign in with Google, your Google account name and email may be used for:

  • Backup and restore of your sightings to Google Drive
  • Community features (optional)

Sign-in is entirely optional. All core features work without it.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data. See Google's Ads Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage analytics to help us understand how the app is used. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google ML Kit: On-device machine learning for cat detection. Processing happens locally; no data is sent to Google for ML Kit processing.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • All photos and sighting data are stored locally in an SQLite database and photo directory on your device. Only sightings you explicitly choose to share are uploaded to our server.
  • Data shared with our API server is transmitted over HTTPS.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

7. Data Retention

  • Local data: Retained on your device until you delete the app or clear its data.
  • Server data: Sighting data shared with the community map is retained as long as the service is active. You can request deletion of your sightings by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

PocketCatGo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app (sightings, photos, settings).
  • Deletion: You can delete individual sightings within the app, or request complete data deletion by contacting us.
  • Opt-out: You can disable ad personalization in your device settings. You can also uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
